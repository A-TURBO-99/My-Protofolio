Who I Am

I'm AMR Mahmoud, known online as A-TURBO-99. I'm a Penetration Tester focused on web application security, API testing, and vulnerability research.

My work sits at the intersection of offensive security and clear reporting — finding real, exploitable issues and explaining them in a way teams can act on. I've reported 60+ vulnerabilities to date, including a recognized disclosure that earned me a spot in the FATORA Bug Bounty Hall of Fame.

I enjoy deep-diving protocols, chaining low-severity bugs into meaningful impact, and continuously sharpening my toolchain.
